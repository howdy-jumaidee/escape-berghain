# Escape Berghain: speaker notes

About 2.5 minutes. Brackets are stage cues. Press N in the deck to see these on screen.

**1. Title**
Good morning. It's Sunday. You're a horse. Nobody told you. [Pause. Let the horse stare at them.] This is Escape Berghain.

**2. Background**
Berghain. The most famous club in the world, and nobody knows what's inside. They put stickers on your phone camera at the door. So we made it up. Three people, 24 hours, zero photos. [Beat.] Honestly, that's how most people remember it anyway.

**3. Purpose**
We wanted a game that feels like a story you tell on Monday. [Let them read the first two bubbles. Don't read them out.] [Deadpan.] Same.

**4. Goal**
Your goal is simple. Get home before you fall into the abyss. Find five lost memories. Your keys are in your jacket. That's where keys always are, and somehow it's still a quest. You can ride the cleaner's mop. And one ending makes you the resident DJ. As a horse. Still easier than getting on the guest list.

**5. Inspiration**
Think Skyrim, but the dragons are bouncers. [Pause. Let it land.] Everyone has something to say, and some of them are lying. Patrick from accounting is definitely not a horse. [Flat German bouncer voice.] Heute nicht. Too many horses already. [Beat.] That's the most realistic line in the game.

**6. Tech stack**
One beat runs everything. Music, strobes, 250 dancers and the DJ booth all follow one 132 BPM clock. The steering gets looser as the night goes on. That's not a bug. That's realism. Dee built it with a team of AI agents. They didn't know what's inside Berghain either.

**7. Future**
Next we go deeper. A labyrinth where the walls shift on the beat. Then the roof. And the basement. [Long pause. Look at the room until it gets uncomfortable.] We don't talk about the basement.

**8. Close**
Some nights you remember. Some nights you don't. Either way, you still have to get home. [Pause.] It's live right now at escapeberghain.dev. No queue. No bouncer. Everyone gets in. Even horses. [Say the URL once more.]
